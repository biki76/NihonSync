**NihonSync (にほんシンク)** is the first comprehensive, free, and offline JLPT N5 
Japanese learning app built specifically for **Bengali (বাংলা)** speakers.

🎌 **Learn Japanese from Zero to N5 — in your mother tongue.**

## 🚀 Features

- ✅ **Complete Alphabet Mastery** — Interactive Hiragana & Katakana charts
- ✅ **100+ N5 Kanji** — HanziWriter stroke order animations + manual tracing
- ✅ **SRS Flashcards** — Spaced Repetition for long-term retention
- ✅ **25 Structured Lessons** — Vocabulary & grammar with Bengali explanations
- ✅ **Offline Audio (TTS)** — High-quality Japanese Text-to-Speech
- ✅ **Interactive Quizzes** — Vocab, Kanji, Grammar with haptic feedback
- ✅ **Cultural Resources** — Counting, time, dates, Jikoshokai, mock interviews
- ✅ **Dark Mode** — Study comfortably day or night
- ✅ **100% Offline** — No internet required after install
- ✅ **Ad-Free** — Zero ads, zero tracking
